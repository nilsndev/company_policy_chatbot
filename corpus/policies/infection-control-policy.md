## Purpose

Defines the minimum infection prevention practices required across MHN clinical settings to protect patients and staff.

## Scope

Applies to all Clinical staff and any employee entering a clinical care area, including Facilities & Operations staff performing maintenance.

## Hand Hygiene

Hand hygiene (alcohol-based hand rub or handwashing) is required before and after every patient contact, before donning gloves, and after removing gloves, following World Health Organization "five moments" guidance as MHN's internal standard.

## Personal Protective Equipment

Appropriate PPE (gloves, gown, mask, eye protection) must be worn based on the isolation precautions posted for a given patient room. Staff must not enter a room under contact or airborne precautions without the required PPE, even briefly.

## Isolation Precautions

Patients with a suspected or confirmed communicable infection are placed under the appropriate isolation precaution level by the treating clinician, with signage posted outside the room per Facilities & Operations coordination.

## Outbreak Response

A cluster of 3 or more suspected related infections on a single unit triggers notification to the Infection Control team and Clinical leadership within 4 hours, who determine whether outbreak protocols apply.

## Related Policies

See the Patient Safety Incident Reporting Policy for reporting infection-control breaches, and the Workplace Safety Policy for general facility safety standards.

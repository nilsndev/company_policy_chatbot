## Purpose

Describes the standard onboarding process for new MHN employees during their first 90 days, so new hires and their managers know what to expect.

## Scope

Applies company-wide to all new full-time and part-time employees. Contractors and volunteers follow an abbreviated version coordinated by their sponsoring department.

## Day One

New hires complete HR paperwork, receive their MHN badge, and are provisioned with a company account and, if applicable, a device, following least-privilege access based on their department and role per the Password and Access Management Policy.

## First Week

New hires complete mandatory company-wide training: the Code of Conduct, the Patient Data Privacy Policy, and the Acceptable Use Policy. Clinical hires additionally complete Infection Control and Patient Safety Incident Reporting training before any patient contact.

## First 30 Days

New hires meet with their manager weekly to review onboarding progress. Department-specific policy training (for example, department-scoped safety or procurement policies) is completed during this window.

## First 90 Days

A 90-day check-in with HR and the manager confirms the new hire has completed all required training and covers any open questions about benefits, including leave accrual under the Leave and Time Off Policy.

## Related Policies

New hires should also review the Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy and the Workplace Safety Policy as part of first-week training.

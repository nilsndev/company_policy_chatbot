## Purpose

Describes paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and unpaid leave available to MHN employees.

## Scope

Applies to all HR-managed leave types company-wide; clinical scheduling constraints are handled through department-specific scheduling processes on top of this baseline.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time staff accrue PTO based on tenure: 15 days per year for staff with under 3 years of tenure, 20 days for 3–7 years, and 25 days for 7+ years. PTO accrues monthly and may be carried over up to 5 days into the next calendar year.

## Sick Leave

All employees receive 8 sick days per year, separate from PTO, usable for personal illness or to care for an immediate family member. A doctor's note is required only for absences of 4 or more consecutive sick days.

## Requesting Leave

PTO requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal for approval by a direct manager. Clinical staff must additionally coordinate with their unit's staffing lead to ensure coverage before a request is finalized.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave beyond accrued PTO and sick leave requires director approval and is generally limited to 30 days except where required by law (see the Parental Leave Policy for parental leave specifically).

## Related Policies

See the Parental Leave Policy for leave related to childbirth, adoption, or foster placement.

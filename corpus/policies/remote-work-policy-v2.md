## Purpose

Defines when and how MHN employees may work remotely, and the technical safeguards required. This version supersedes Remote Work Policy v1 and adds an explicit international remote work process after several ad hoc requests surfaced gaps in the previous version.

## Scope

Applies to employees in roles that do not require physical presence at an MHN facility, at manager discretion.

## Eligibility

Remote work must be approved in writing by a direct manager. Clinical roles that require direct patient contact are generally not eligible. Fully remote arrangements longer than 30 consecutive days require director-level approval in addition to the manager's.

## Location — Domestic

Employees may work remotely from within the United States without additional approval beyond their manager's, as long as the role is remote-eligible.

## Location — International

Working from outside the United States for more than two consecutive weeks requires a completed International Remote Work Request, approved by both the manager and IT & Security at least 10 business days in advance, due to data residency, tax, and network access concerns. Short trips of under two weeks (for example, a working vacation) only need manager sign-off, but must still use VPN for any system access, and employees should expect degraded video-call quality on some international networks.

## Technical Requirements

Remote work requires a company-issued laptop, VPN connection for any access to internal systems, and MFA per the Password and Access Management Policy. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with VPN active. International remote work additionally requires IT & Security to confirm the destination country is not on the current restricted-network list before departure.

## Related Policies

See the Acceptable Use Policy for general device rules that apply whether working remotely or on-site, and the Password and Access Management Policy for MFA requirements.

## Purpose

Sets the rules for issuance and use of MHN corporate credit cards.

## Scope

Applies to employees issued a corporate card, typically managers and above, or staff with recurring purchasing needs approved by a director.

## Eligibility and Issuance

Corporate cards are issued by Finance on manager request with director approval. Cards are tied to an individual employee and must not be shared or used by anyone else, including coworkers covering for an absent employee.

## Permitted Use

Corporate cards may be used for approved business expenses and vendor purchases within the requesting employee's approved threshold under the Procurement and Vendor Policy. Personal purchases on a corporate card are prohibited, even if reimbursed immediately.

## Reconciliation

Cardholders must reconcile transactions in the expense system within 15 days of each statement close, attaching receipts consistent with the Travel and Expense Policy's documentation requirements.

## Card Loss or Suspected Fraud

Report a lost card or suspected fraudulent charge to Finance and the card issuer immediately; Finance will cancel and reissue the card. Delayed reporting beyond 24 hours may make the cardholder responsible for fraudulent charges under the issuer's terms.

## Termination

Corporate cards are deactivated immediately when an employee changes roles to a non-eligible position or leaves MHN, coordinated with the Password and Access Management Policy's offboarding process.

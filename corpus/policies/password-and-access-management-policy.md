## Purpose

Sets minimum requirements for passwords, multi-factor authentication, and account access reviews across MHN systems.

## Scope

Applies to all employees with any MHN system account, and specifically to IT & Security staff who provision and review access.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters, unique to MHN systems (not reused from personal accounts), and changed immediately if suspected compromised. MHN does not require periodic forced rotation for compliant passwords, consistent with current security best practice.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is required for all clinical systems, email, and remote access (VPN). Employees must not share MFA codes or approve a push notification they did not initiate themselves.

## Access Provisioning

New account access is granted based on department and role, following least-privilege — an employee should have access to exactly what their job requires, no more. Access requests outside the standard department/role template require manager approval.

## Access Review

IT & Security runs a quarterly access review with each department manager to confirm access still matches current role. Access for terminated employees is revoked within one hour of the termination being processed by HR.

## Related Policies

See the Incident Response Policy for what happens after a suspected account compromise, and the Acceptable Use Policy for general credential-sharing rules.

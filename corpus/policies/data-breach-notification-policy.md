## Purpose

Sets the timeline and responsibilities for notifying regulators, patients, and leadership when a data breach involving PHI or other Restricted data occurs.

## Scope

Applies to all employees who discover or are informed of a suspected breach, and specifically to Compliance & Risk staff who own the response process.

## Immediate Reporting

Any employee who suspects a breach must notify Compliance & Risk within one hour of discovery, using the Breach Hotline or the incident form linked from the intranet homepage. Do not wait to confirm the breach is real before reporting — false alarms are expected and welcomed.

## Investigation

Compliance & Risk, together with IT & Security per the Incident Response Policy, will investigate scope and root cause within 72 hours of the initial report, consistent with HIPAA-style breach notification timelines used as MHN's internal standard.

## Notification

If PHI for 500 or more individuals is confirmed affected, Compliance & Risk will notify affected patients, MHN leadership, and relevant regulators within the required window. For smaller breaches, affected individuals are still notified, on a rolling basis as confirmed.

## Documentation

Every breach, regardless of size, is logged in the Compliance & Risk incident register, including root cause and remediation steps, for the annual compliance audit.

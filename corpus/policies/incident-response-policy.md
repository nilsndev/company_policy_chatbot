## Purpose

Defines how MHN detects, contains, and recovers from security incidents, including malware, unauthorized access, and lost devices.

## Scope

Applies to IT & Security staff who lead response, and to all employees who must report suspected incidents.

## Reporting

Any employee who suspects a security incident — a phishing email that was clicked, a lost laptop, unusual account activity — must report it to the IT & Security Help Desk immediately, available 24/7. Do not attempt to investigate or "clean up" a suspected compromise yourself.

## Triage and Containment

IT & Security triages every report within 30 minutes during business hours (2 hours off-hours). Confirmed incidents are contained by isolating affected accounts or devices from the network as the first response step, before root-cause analysis begins.

## PHI Involvement

If an incident may have exposed PHI, IT & Security immediately loops in Compliance & Risk per the Data Breach Notification Policy — the two teams run a joint response, IT & Security on the technical containment, Compliance & Risk on regulatory and patient notification.

## Post-Incident Review

Every confirmed incident gets a written post-incident review within five business days, covering root cause, timeline, and remediation, filed with IT & Security leadership.

## Related Policies

See the Patient Data Privacy Policy for PHI handling rules and the Password and Access Management Policy for the credential rotation required after a suspected account compromise.

## Purpose

MHN is committed to a workplace free of harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. This policy defines prohibited conduct and the reporting and investigation process.

## Scope

Applies company-wide to all employees, contractors, and volunteers, and covers conduct at MHN facilities, MHN-sponsored events, and in work-related communications.

## Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited conduct includes unwelcome sexual advances, offensive jokes or comments about a protected characteristic, intimidation, and retaliation against anyone who reports or participates in an investigation of a complaint under this policy.

## Reporting

Employees may report concerns to their manager, any HR representative, or the confidential Compliance & Risk hotline. Employees are not required to report to their direct manager if the manager is the subject of the concern.

## Investigation

HR investigates every report within 5 business days of receipt. Both parties are informed of the outcome to the extent permitted by privacy considerations. Investigations are kept confidential to the extent possible while allowing a thorough review.

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against a good-faith reporter or witness is a separate, serious violation of this policy and will be treated at least as seriously as the underlying complaint.

## Purpose

Describes paid parental leave available to MHN employees following childbirth, adoption, or foster placement, supplementing the baseline Leave and Time Off Policy.

## Scope

Applies to all full-time employees company-wide who have completed at least 90 days of employment (see the Employee Onboarding Policy for the onboarding timeline).

## Paid Parental Leave

Eligible employees receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave, usable within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement. Both parents at MHN are eligible independently, regardless of gender.

## Coordination with Other Leave

Parental leave runs concurrently with, not in addition to, any legally mandated unpaid leave. Employees may use accrued PTO under the Leave and Time Off Policy to extend time away beyond the 8 paid weeks, subject to manager approval.

## Requesting Leave

Employees should notify HR and their manager at least 30 days before the expected start of leave when possible. For clinical staff, the unit staffing lead is looped in to plan coverage well in advance.

## Return to Work

Employees are guaranteed return to the same or an equivalent role and pay level. A phased return (reduced hours for up to 2 weeks) is available on request.

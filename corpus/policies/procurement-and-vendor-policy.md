## Purpose

Defines how MHN departments purchase goods and services from outside vendors, and the approval thresholds involved.

## Scope

Applies company-wide to any employee initiating a purchase from an external vendor on behalf of MHN.

## Purchase Thresholds

Purchases under $1,000 may be approved by a direct manager. Purchases from $1,000 to $25,000 require director approval and a documented comparison of at least two vendor quotes. Purchases over $25,000 require a formal request-for-proposal process managed by Finance procurement.

## Vendor Onboarding

Any new vendor must complete Finance's vendor onboarding, including a W-9 (or equivalent) and, for any vendor who will handle PHI, a signed Business Associate Agreement coordinated with Compliance & Risk per the Patient Data Privacy Policy.

## Preferred Vendors

Departments should check the current preferred-vendor list published by Finance before soliciting new quotes — preferred vendors have pre-negotiated rates and simplified onboarding.

## Conflicts of Interest

Any employee with a personal or financial relationship with a prospective vendor must disclose it before initiating the purchase, per the Code of Conduct.

## Related Policies

See the Travel and Expense Policy for individual expense reimbursement, and the Corporate Card Policy for card-based purchasing specifically.

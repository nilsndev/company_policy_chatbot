## Purpose

Sets the standard verification steps for administering medication to patients, to prevent administration errors.

## Scope

Applies to all Clinical staff authorized to administer medication across MHN facilities.

## The Five Rights

Before administering any medication, staff must verify the five rights: right patient (two identifiers, e.g. name and date of birth), right medication, right dose, right route, and right time. Verification must happen at the bedside, immediately before administration, not earlier in the workflow.

## High-Alert Medications

Medications on MHN's high-alert list (insulin, anticoagulants, opioids, and others published by Clinical leadership) require independent double-check by a second qualified staff member before administration.

## Documentation

Administration must be documented in the electronic medication administration record (eMAR) immediately after giving the medication, never in advance and never batched at the end of a shift. See the Clinical Documentation Standards Policy for general documentation timeliness rules.

## Reporting Errors

Any medication error or near miss — wrong dose, wrong time, omitted dose — must be reported per the Patient Safety Incident Reporting Policy, regardless of whether the patient was harmed.

## Related Policies

See the Patient Safety Incident Reporting Policy for how to report an error, and Clinical Documentation Standards Policy for eMAR requirements.

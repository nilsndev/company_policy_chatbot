## Purpose

Defines the four data sensitivity tiers used across MHN systems so that employees can apply the right handling rules without having to ask Compliance & Risk case by case.

## Scope

Applies company-wide to all data created, received, or stored on MHN systems, including email, shared drives, and clinical applications.

## Classification Tiers

**Restricted** — protected health information (PHI), social security numbers, payment card data. Requires encryption at rest and in transit, and access logging.

**Confidential** — internal financial data, unreleased policy drafts, employee personnel files. Shared only on a need-to-know basis within MHN.

**Internal** — general internal communications, meeting notes, internal wikis. Not for external sharing but low individual risk if exposed.

**Public** — approved marketing materials, published policies, job postings. Safe for external distribution once approved by Communications.

## Handling Rules

Restricted and Confidential data may not be sent to personal email addresses. Only Restricted data requires the encrypted email gateway; Confidential data may use standard MHN email within the organization. All four tiers must respect the retention schedules published by Compliance & Risk.

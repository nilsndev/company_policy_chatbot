## Purpose

Describes how employees book work travel and submit business expenses for reimbursement.

## Scope

Applies company-wide to any employee incurring work-related travel or business expenses.

## Booking Travel

Air travel and hotels should be booked through MHN's designated travel portal when available. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; director approval is required for business class on longer flights.

## Reimbursable Expenses

Reasonable meals, ground transportation, and lodging while traveling for work are reimbursable. Alcohol is reimbursable only when part of a client or vendor meal, up to a modest per-person limit published by Finance, and must be itemized separately.

## Submitting Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, with itemized receipts for any single expense over $25. Expenses submitted after 90 days will generally not be reimbursed absent an exceptional circumstance approved by Finance leadership.

## Approval

Expense reports are approved by the employee's direct manager. Expenses over $2,000 in a single report require additional director approval.

## Related Policies

See the Procurement and Vendor Policy for purchasing goods or services from a vendor, as distinct from individual employee expense reimbursement.

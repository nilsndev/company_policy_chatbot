## Purpose

Defines when and how MHN employees may work remotely, and the technical safeguards required.

## Scope

Applies to employees in roles that do not require physical presence at an MHN facility, at manager discretion.

## Eligibility

Remote work must be approved in writing by a direct manager. Clinical roles that require direct patient contact are generally not eligible.

## Location

Employees may work remotely from within the United States. Working from outside the country requires separate written approval from both the manager and IT & Security, due to data residency and network access concerns.

## Technical Requirements

Remote work requires a company-issued laptop, VPN connection for any access to internal systems, and MFA per the Password and Access Management Policy. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with VPN active.

## Related Policies

See the Acceptable Use Policy for general device rules that apply whether working remotely or on-site.

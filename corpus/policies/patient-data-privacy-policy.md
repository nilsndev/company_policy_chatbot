## Purpose

MHN handles protected health information (PHI) for approximately 4,200 employees' worth of patients across six facilities. This policy defines how PHI must be accessed, stored, transmitted, and disposed of, consistent with our HIPAA-style obligations.

## Scope

Applies company-wide. Clinical staff who directly access patient records have additional obligations under the Clinical Documentation Standards Policy and Infection Control Policy.

## Minimum Necessary Access

Employees may only access the PHI required to perform their specific job function. Browsing patient records out of curiosity ("snooping"), including records of coworkers, family members, or public figures, is strictly prohibited and is grounds for immediate termination.

## Storage and Transmission

PHI must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage. PHI transmitted by email must use MHN's encrypted email gateway. Printed PHI must be shredded, not placed in regular trash or recycling.

## Third Parties

Any vendor or contractor who will handle PHI must have a signed Business Associate Agreement on file with Compliance & Risk before access is granted.

## Breach Response

Any suspected exposure of PHI — a lost device, misdirected fax, or unauthorized access — must be reported immediately per the Data Breach Notification Policy. Do not attempt to "quietly fix" a suspected breach without reporting it first.

## Related Policies

See the Data Classification Policy for how PHI is labeled, and the Incident Response Policy for the technical response process when a breach involves IT systems.

## Purpose

Sets baseline safety practices for MHN facilities, covering both clinical and non-clinical work areas.

## Scope

Applies company-wide; clinical areas have additional requirements under the Infection Control Policy.

## Hazard Reporting

Any workplace hazard — a spill, a broken fixture, blocked emergency exit — must be reported to Facilities & Operations immediately through the facilities ticket system or, for urgent hazards, by phone to the facilities hotline.

## Ergonomics

Employees with primarily desk-based roles may request an ergonomic assessment from Facilities & Operations at any time, and are encouraged to do so within their first 30 days per the Employee Onboarding Policy.

## Lifting and Physical Safety

Manual lifting over 50 lbs requires a team lift or mechanical assistance (available at every facility). Clinical staff should refer to unit-specific patient-handling guidance for safe patient transfer and repositioning.

## Incident Reporting

Any workplace injury, regardless of severity, must be reported to a manager and Facilities & Operations within 24 hours using the workplace injury report form, separate from the Patient Safety Incident Reporting Policy, which covers patient-facing events.

## Related Policies

See the Emergency Preparedness Policy for evacuation and emergency procedures, and the Infection Control Policy for clinical-area-specific safety requirements.

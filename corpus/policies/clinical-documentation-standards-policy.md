## Purpose

Sets minimum standards for timely, accurate documentation in the electronic health record (EHR), which is both a clinical care tool and a legal record.

## Scope

Applies to all Clinical staff who document patient care at any MHN facility.

## Timeliness

Clinical notes must be completed within 24 hours of the encounter. Medication administration must be documented immediately per the Medication Administration Safety Policy — the 24-hour window does not apply to eMAR entries.

## Accuracy and Amendments

Notes must reflect what was actually observed and done. Copy-forward ("carry forward") of prior notes is permitted only when reviewed and updated to reflect the current encounter — unreviewed copy-forward that misrepresents the current visit is a documentation integrity violation. Corrections to a signed note must be made as a dated addendum, never by editing or deleting the original entry.

## Access and Privacy

EHR access follows minimum-necessary access under the Patient Data Privacy Policy. Every EHR access is logged and subject to periodic audit by Compliance & Risk.

## Related Policies

See the Medication Administration Safety Policy for eMAR-specific timing, and the Patient Data Privacy Policy for access and privacy rules governing the EHR.

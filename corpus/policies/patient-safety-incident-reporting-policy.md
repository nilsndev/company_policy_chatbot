## Purpose

Establishes a non-punitive process for reporting patient safety events — near misses, adverse events, and sentinel events — so MHN can learn from them and improve care.

## Scope

Applies to all Clinical staff (Nursing and Physicians) across all six MHN facilities.

## What to Report

Report any event that did or could have harmed a patient: a medication error (see the Medication Administration Safety Policy), a fall, a wrong-site procedure, or a near miss caught before reaching the patient. Near misses are just as important to report as actual harm events.

## How to Report

Events are reported through the Patient Safety Event system within 24 hours of the event or discovery, whichever is later. Reports may be submitted anonymously, though including your name helps the review team follow up with questions.

## Non-Punitive Review

MHN's Patient Safety Committee reviews every report to identify systemic causes, not to assign individual blame. Reporting an event you were involved in will not itself result in discipline, except in cases of reckless disregard for patient safety or a pattern of repeated preventable errors.

## Sentinel Events

A sentinel event — one resulting in death or serious harm — triggers an immediate root-cause analysis within 5 business days, led by the Patient Safety Committee with Clinical leadership.

## Related Policies

See the Medication Administration Safety Policy and Infection Control Policy for the specific clinical safety standards this reporting process supports.

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of professional behavior expected of everyone at Meridian Health Network (MHN), across all hospitals, clinics, and administrative sites.

## Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and volunteers company-wide, regardless of department or role.

## Policy

Employees must treat patients, visitors, and colleagues with respect and dignity. Employees must not discriminate on the basis of race, gender, religion, disability, or any other protected characteristic. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to a manager before accepting outside employment or board positions related to healthcare vendors.

Employees may not use MHN resources — email, badge access, or patient records systems — for personal commercial purposes.

## Reporting Violations

Suspected violations should be reported to a manager, HR, or the Compliance & Risk hotline. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation of this policy.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in coaching, formal discipline, or termination, depending on severity, at management's discretion in consultation with HR.

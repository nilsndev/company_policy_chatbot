## Purpose

Defines how MHN badge access is provisioned, used, and revoked across all six facilities.

## Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and volunteers issued an MHN badge.

## Provisioning

Badge access is provisioned based on department and role, following the same least-privilege principle as system access under the Password and Access Management Policy — an employee's badge grants access to the areas their job requires, not the whole facility by default.

## Use

Badges are non-transferable; employees must not lend a badge to anyone, including a coworker who forgot theirs (use the visitor/temporary badge process at the front desk instead). Tailgating — following someone through a badge-controlled door without badging in yourself — is prohibited, including for coworkers.

## Restricted Areas

Areas such as the pharmacy, server rooms, and NICU require additional access approval beyond standard department provisioning, granted by the area's Clinical or IT & Security lead as applicable.

## Loss and Termination

A lost badge must be reported to Facilities & Operations immediately for deactivation and reissuance. Badges are deactivated within one hour of a termination being processed by HR, coordinated with the Password and Access Management Policy's system-access offboarding.

## Related Policies

See the Password and Access Management Policy for system account access, and the Emergency Preparedness Policy for badge behavior during an emergency evacuation.

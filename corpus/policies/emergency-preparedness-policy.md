## Purpose

Describes MHN's emergency response procedures for fire, severe weather, active threat, and other facility-wide emergencies.

## Scope

Applies company-wide to all employees, contractors, and volunteers at any MHN facility.

## Evacuation

Each facility posts evacuation routes on every floor. On a fire alarm, employees must evacuate immediately via the nearest posted route and gather at the designated assembly point — do not stop to badge out or collect personal items. Clinical staff follow unit-specific patient evacuation procedures, which take priority over general staff evacuation.

## Severe Weather

Facilities & Operations issues shelter-in-place notices for severe weather via the emergency notification system (text, email, and intercom). Employees should move to designated shelter areas away from exterior windows.

## Active Threat

MHN follows a run-hide-fight framework for active threat situations, covered in mandatory annual training. Call 911 as soon as safely possible; do not assume someone else has called.

## Notification System

All employees are automatically enrolled in the emergency notification system using their MHN contact information on file; opting out is not permitted for safety-critical alerts.

## Related Policies

See the Workplace Safety Policy for general hazard reporting, and the Facility Access and Badge Policy for badge behavior during an evacuation (badges are not required to exit during an emergency).

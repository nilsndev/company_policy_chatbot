## Purpose

Defines acceptable use of MHN-owned computers, network, email, and cloud accounts.

## Scope

Applies company-wide to every employee, contractor, and volunteer issued MHN credentials or a device.

## Acceptable Use

MHN systems are provided for work purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use (checking personal email, brief personal calls) is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work or violate any other MHN policy.

## Prohibited Use

Employees may not: install unapproved software on MHN devices, use MHN accounts for personal business ventures, share login credentials with anyone (including coworkers), or connect personal USB storage devices to clinical workstations.

## Monitoring

MHN reserves the right to monitor network traffic, email, and device activity for security and compliance purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy in MHN systems.

## Device Loss

A lost or stolen MHN device must be reported to IT & Security within one hour, per the Incident Response Policy, so it can be remotely locked.

## Related Policies

See the Remote Work Policy for rules specific to working outside an MHN facility, and the Password and Access Management Policy for credential requirements.

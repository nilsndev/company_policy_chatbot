## Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of professional behavior expected of everyone at Meridian Health Network (MHN), across all hospitals, clinics, and administrative sites. This version supersedes Code of Conduct v1 and adds an explicit social media clause and a clearer conflict-of-interest disclosure process.

## Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and volunteers company-wide, regardless of department or role.

## Policy

Employees must treat patients, visitors, and colleagues with respect and dignity. Employees must not discriminate on the basis of race, gender, religion, disability, or any other protected characteristic. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to a manager and to HR, using the Conflict of Interest Disclosure form, before accepting outside employment or board positions related to healthcare vendors, and annually thereafter.

Employees may not use MHN resources — email, badge access, or patient records systems — for personal commercial purposes.

## Social Media

Employees may identify as MHN employees on personal social media but must not post protected patient information, must not speak on MHN's behalf without authorization from Communications, and should add a disclaimer that views are their own when discussing healthcare topics publicly.

## Reporting Violations

Suspected violations should be reported to a manager, HR, or the Compliance & Risk hotline (available 24/7, anonymous reporting supported). Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation of this policy and will be treated as a separate, aggravating offense.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in coaching, formal discipline, or termination, depending on severity, at management's discretion in consultation with HR and, for compliance-related matters, Compliance & Risk.
